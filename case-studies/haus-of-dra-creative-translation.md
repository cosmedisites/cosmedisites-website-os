# Case study: HAUS of DRA, Creative Translation (October 2026)

The project that exposed the gap Stage 05T and 05V now close. Internal reference only; no client data beyond what is needed to explain the method.

## What happened

| Version | What it was | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| v1 prototype | Strategically sound: HAUS as master brand, Clinic and Academy routes, consultation-first framework, proof scoping, compliant treatment template. Ivory/editorial look, thin architectural rules, uniform cards. | Strategy approved; visually generic |
| v2 keyframes | Better colour balance and stronger brand cues (black, ivory, brass). | Still looked AI/system-generated |
| Creative reset | Audience (Instagram/TikTok-led), the clinic's real interiors (black slatted walls, gold emblem, monochrome signage, white clinical rooms, black uniforms), its social feed, the competitor bar and the real capability of Elementor Atomic were all taken into account. | Direction approved |
| Concepts A to C | High-fidelity moments using real photography: cinematic moving hero; visual treatment discovery with provider labels; results as content (before/after compare); personality-led team; energetic Academy; "Inside HAUS" bridging site and social. | Approved, then built natively and QA'd before roll-out |

## What survived the creative reset (the LOCKED list)

Semantic architecture, service discovery by treatment and concern, local SEO, HTML content, internal linking, conversion CTAs, proof scoping, mobile booking, the HAUS Team vs independent Collective distinction, and compliance gates.

## Lessons encoded in v1.1

1. The brand was more obvious from the premises, uniforms, signage, transformations, people and social content than from the staging website. A website-only audit would have missed it. (Stage 01 and 05 sources.)
2. The component system had been designed before the creative was right, so the site looked like a component library. (Stage 05T before 06.)
3. Placeholders nearly dictated the layout before real photography arrived. (Asset gate at 05V.)
4. Motion that ships must be pausable and respect reduced-motion; Elementor's native Interactions do neither on their own. (05V collision test, 12F.)
5. Services delivered by independent businesses need a visible provider line so discovery stays broad without implying the clinic delivers them. (05V provider clarity check.)
