# Stage 8 — Client Strategy Review

**Purpose:** Turn the approved internal strategy into a concise client presentation that earns decisions rather than vague feedback.

## 8A — Client Strategy Deck Generator

**Use when:** When strategy, IA and visual direction are sufficiently mature.  
**Inputs:** Project context; positioning; Current→Proposed; mockups; proof; page system.  
**Produces:** 12–16 slide deck outline/copy.

```text
Create the client-facing CosmediSites Website Redesign Strategy Review.

Default sequence:
01 Cover / positioning statement
02 The aim
03 What changes: current → proposed
04 What this redesign is designed to improve
05 Visual system
06 Homepage / first impression
07 User journey / discovery
08 Distinctive brand idea / methodology
09 Priority service/category if strategically important
10 Clinical team / brand architecture
11 Treatment / service page system
12 Trust / results
13 Mobile / booking
14 Photography / content
15 What we need you to approve
16 Once approved: inputs + next steps

For each slide output:
SLIDE NUMBER
HEADLINE
ONE-LINE JOB
CLIENT-FACING COPY
VISUAL / MOCKUP TO SHOW
DECISION REQUIRED? Yes/No

Rules:
- One idea per slide.
- One sentence explains why; the visual proves it.
- Use client language, not internal terminology.
- Protect strong approved positioning lines.
- Do not introduce new strategy inside the deck.
- Do not show several arbitrary directions if one is recommended.
```

## 8B — Deck Compression / Edit

**Use when:** When the draft deck is too long or text-heavy.  
**Inputs:** Draft deck.  
**Produces:** Tighter client deck preserving strategic content.

```text
Edit the supplied client deck for decision clarity.

For every slide ask:
- What is the single idea?
- Does the client need this to make a decision?
- Is this internal process language?
- Can the mockup be larger?
- Is the same point made elsewhere?

Return:
KEEP
MERGE WITH [slide]
CUT
REWRITE

Then provide revised copy for slides that need it.

Targets:
- roughly 12–16 slides unless the project genuinely needs more;
- no paragraph should exist if a sentence will do;
- visuals should dominate UI demonstration slides.
```

## 8C — Presenter Notes

**Use when:** Before the strategy review meeting.  
**Inputs:** Approved client deck.  
**Produces:** Speaker notes and decision prompts.

```text
Write concise presenter notes for the client strategy review.

For each slide:
- WHAT TO SAY — 2–5 bullets.
- WHY IT MATTERS — one sentence.
- QUESTION / DECISION — only if the slide needs client input.
- DO NOT GET DRAWN INTO — likely low-level preference discussion that should not derail the strategic decision.

Tone:
calm, confident, collaborative.
Do not read the slide verbatim.
Do not apologise for making a recommendation.
```

## 8D — Client Deck Red Team

**Use when:** Before sending/presenting.  
**Inputs:** Draft deck; approved strategy.  
**Produces:** Red-team QA.

```text
Red-team the client strategy deck.

Answer:
1. What commercial problem is being solved?
2. What is the recommended positioning?
3. How will patients use the site differently?
4. Where is trust/proof demonstrated?
5. How does the site support future growth?
6. What business outcomes are intended?
7. What exactly is the client being asked to approve?
8. What inputs are needed next?
9. What happens after approval?
10. Could this deck be sent to another clinic unchanged?

For each question: CLEAR / WEAK / MISSING.

Also flag:
- design jargon;
- unsupported claims;
- fake precision;
- duplicated slides;
- tiny mockups;
- new strategy introduced without evidence.

Finish PASS / FAIL / BLOCKED.
```
