## Allowed types

Qualification · Registration · Membership · Award · Review · Patient result · Media · Teaching / speaking · Original data / study · Other

## Rules

Never infer a credential from a biography. Never rewrite a testimonial as though verbatim. Never claim an award without the source. Separate practitioner proof from clinic proof. Flag expired proof.
